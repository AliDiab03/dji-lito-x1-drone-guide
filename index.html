Questions people ask first

Short answers before you install.

Is this an official DJI app?

No. This is an independent, unofficial guide. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by DJI. Product names and trademarks belong to their owners and are used only to identify the drone the guide is about.

Can I fly my drone with this app?

No. The app is a guide and does not connect to or control the drone. To fly, use the official app from the manufacturer. This guide helps you prepare, learn and troubleshoot.

Does it need internet?

The guides and the PDF manual are stored in the app. An internet connection is used to show ads and to open outside pages such as the manufacturer's website or store.

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free and supported by ads.

What data does the app collect?

The app has no accounts and does not ask for personal details. Read the full privacy policy to see what the ad provider may process.
