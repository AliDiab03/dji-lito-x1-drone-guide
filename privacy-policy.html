Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what information the DJI Lito X1 Drone Guide app ("the app", "we", "us") handles, how advertising works in the app, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

The short version

  • The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may process your device's advertising ID and similar technical data to serve and measure ads.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings at any time.
  • Your checklist and step progress stays on your device only.

1. Who we are

The app is published by MOHA DEV, an independent developer. You can contact us at allouse.o2003@gmail.com.

The app is an unofficial guide and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by DJI.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information directly. We do not run our own servers for the app and we do not create user profiles.

Information stored on your device

The app saves small settings, such as which checklist items or steps you have marked as done, in the app's private storage on your device. This information is not sent to us. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Information processed by advertising partners

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads. When ads are requested, Google and its partners may automatically collect and process the following, depending on your device settings and the laws that apply to you:

3. Your ad choices

You can limit or turn off ad personalization at any time:

  • On your device: open Android Settings and look for Google, then Ads (the exact path varies by device) to reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization.
  • On the web: manage your preferences at adssettings.google.com.

If you opt out of personalized ads, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

4. Permissions

The app requests only these Android permissions:

  • Internet to load ads and open pages you tap.
  • Network state to check whether a connection is available.
  • Advertising ID (added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK) to let the ad provider serve and measure ads.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, or precise location.

5. Links to other websites

The app can open outside pages, such as the manufacturer's website and online store. These pages open inside the app or in your browser. We do not control those services and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please read their policies before using them.

6. Offline content

The guides and the PDF manual are stored inside the app. Reading them does not send any information about you to us.

7. Children's privacy

The app is a general audience guide and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age set by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help resolve it.

8. Data retention and security

We do not hold personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. Locally stored settings remain on your device until you clear them. Data processed by Google is kept under Google's own policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to certain processing, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising. Because we do not hold your personal data, most requests about ad-related data are handled through Google using the controls described in section 3. You can also contact us at allouse.o2003@gmail.com and we will help point you in the right direction.
